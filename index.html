A better way to choose

Match the tool to the job.

01

Want to stay in your editor?

Try Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf, or Continue. Compare how each handles your language, repository, and team policy.

02

Need an agent across a codebase?

Try Claude Code, Codex, or Gemini CLI. Review plans, diffs, shell access, and permission controls before broad use.

03

Want to prototype an app fast?

Try Grok Build or editor agents. Test the generated app, inspect dependencies, and keep a clean version-control checkpoint.
